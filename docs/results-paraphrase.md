# Paraphrase test results (transfer gap: memory vs application)

Reproducible check that the Vantage transfer-gap functions surface the fluency illusion per section and per concept, and abstain when data is thin. Generated by `vantage_tools/evaluate_paraphrase.py`; re-running reproduces these numbers exactly.

## What is under test

- `scoring.transfer_gap` (per section) and `scoring.concept_transfer_gaps` (per concept), reused as shipped, with gates from `ScoringConfig` (min_outcomes_transfer=10, min_outcomes_transfer_card=3, fluency_gap_threshold=0.15).

## Pre-registration (metric + learner model)

- Metric: gap = recall_on_card - accuracy_on_reworded_items, reported per SECTION and per CONCEPT.
- Flag rule: a concept is fluency-illusion flagged iff gap >= 0.15 with >= 3 reworded outcomes; fewer outcomes means abstain.
- Learner model (seeded, SEED=20260702): per concept a latent mastery m in [0.62, 0.96]; recall ~ Bernoulli(m) over [30, 50] reviews; reworded accuracy ~ Bernoulli(clamp(m - transfer_penalty)) over [30, 60] attempts. transfer_penalty = 0.3 (constant illusion magnitude) is applied to a seeded 50% of concepts (transfer-prone); the rest transfer cleanly (penalty 0), producing both gapped and null concepts. ['bb_membrane_transport', 'cp_acid_base'] are starved to 2 outcomes to force the abstention path.

> This is a SIMULATED cohort. The numbers validate the pipeline and the gap formulas, not a real student population.

## Headline

- 30 concepts scored, 13 flagged for the fluency illusion, 17 null (no gap), 2 abstained (insufficient data).
- Self-validation vs the seeded ground truth (scored concepts): TP=13, FP=0, FN=1, TN=16, accuracy=0.967.

- Per-concept caught what the section average hid: the Chem/Phys section is not flagged, yet it contains flagged concept(s): cp_kinematics, cp_ohms_law.
